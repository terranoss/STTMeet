1. Action Minutes TemplateShort Description: A lean, task-driven record focusing strictly on decisions and assigned deliverables.   Explanation: Action minutes discard the debate and back-and-forth conversation, documenting only the final decisions reached and the tangible outcomes. The central feature is an actionable task register identifying who owns each task, the due date, and the current status. It often begins with a status review of previous action items to maintain accountability. This format is best suited for weekly operational syncs, agile team standups, and fast-paced project updates where speed and clear ownership are paramount.   2. Discussion (Topical) Minutes TemplateShort Description: A thematic summary designed to capture the rationale, debate, and perspectives behind strategic choices.   Explanation: Discussion minutes go beyond recording what was decided by documenting why it was decided. Organized by agenda topic, they capture the context presented, arguments in favor, counterpoints or risks raised, and any alternative solutions considered before reaching a consensus. This approach provides historical context so future stakeholders understand the reasoning behind policies or strategic pivots. It is best suited for strategy committees, policy task forces, and steering groups.   3. Formal Board / Corporate Governance Minutes TemplateShort Description: A structured legal document recording official proceedings, parliamentary motions, and voting outcomes.   Explanation: Board minutes serve as an official legal record demonstrating that directors exercised their fiduciary duties properly. They follow parliamentary procedure and document legal formalities such as the call to order, quorum confirmation, approval of previous minutes, exact resolution texts, movers, seconders, voting counts, and conflict-of-interest disclosures. The record concludes with formal signatures from the Corporate Secretary and Board Chairperson. It is used for Board of Directors meetings, Annual General Meetings (AGMs), and statutory compliance audits.   4. Three-Column Operational Minutes TemplateShort Description: A structured, side-by-side tabular log that connects topics directly with actions and owners.   Explanation: This format organizes the entire meeting into a simple three-part grid: Item & Deliberation (Column 1), Action Required & Decision (Column 2), and Responsibility & Deadline (Column 3). By placing discussion points, agreed-upon fixes, and specific accountable individuals on the exact same row, it eliminates misunderstandings between separate departments or external vendors. It is particularly effective for construction site walk-throughs, multi-party vendor coordination, and engineering reviews.
